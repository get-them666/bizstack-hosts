Recommended Strategy — Build a 3-Tier Bench

Free channels first, job boards when volume grows, day labor only for emergencies

1

Your Vetted Subs · do this now

Recruit 2–3 part-time cleaners via Facebook groups + Craigslist while you have time. Run a photo-verified trial on your own property first — it filters out the flaky 60% and doubles as training. Free, and they're your go-to on every job.

2

Indeed Hiring · when volume is recurring

Post the template below when you have steady turnover volume to sustain a regular hire. Respond to applicants same day — good cleaners get snatched fast.

3

Day-Labor Backup · emergencies only

PeopleReady / Instawork / Wonolo cost a premium per worker. Keep their numbers handy for same-day gaps when every sub is booked — not as the week-to-week answer.

Indeed Job Posting Template

Copy-paste ready — edit address/availability before posting

Turnover Cleaning — Short-Term Rental (Hampton Roads)

📍 Location: [YOUR AREA]

💰 Pay: $XX/hour or $XX/turnover

📅 Schedule: As-needed, with 1–2 day notice

We're a short-term rental operations company managing vacation rentals in Hampton Roads. We're looking for reliable, detail-oriented cleaners for guest turnovers.

What you'll do:

  • Turnover cleaning between guest stays (2–4hr blocks)
  • Strip/make beds, clean bathrooms, kitchen, and floors
  • Check linens, restock supplies, report damages via photo

Requirements:

  • Reliable transportation within Hampton Roads
  • Can lift up to 30 lbs, bend/stoop for cleaning tasks
  • Available mornings/midday on short notice
  • Phone with camera (for reporting/communications)

No experience required — we provide cleaning checklist and supplies. Perfect for flexible scheduling.

Reply with your availability and nearest city in Hampton Roads.

💡 Tips: Post as "Paid" in Indeed's free tier. Respond to applicants same day — quality cleaners get snatched fast. Track applicants in Broom Service Docs.
