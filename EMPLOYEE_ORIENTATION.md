# Broom Service — Employee Orientation & Time Clock Guide

Welcome to the team! This guide walks you through how Broom Service works, how to
use your cleaner portal, and — most importantly — how the time clock works, so
everyone gets paid correctly and on time.

---

## 1. Our Business in 30 Seconds

Broom Service runs **short-term rental (STR) cleaning and co-hosting**. In plain English:

- Homeowners rent out their properties on Airbnb / Vrbo (these are "short-term rentals").
- Between guests, the property must be **turned over** — cleaned and reset to 5-star standard.
- That turnover cleaning is the job you do. Co-hosting is the whole package (guest
  messaging, check-in, cleaning, maintenance) run through Broom Service.

Your job = show up on time, do a great turnover, and clock in and out honestly.

---

## 2. Your Cleaner Account

- You get a **4-digit PIN** from your manager.
- Open **bizstackperks.com/worker-login** on your phone (or scan the QR from your manager).
- Enter your **phone number** and your **PIN**. That's it — you're in.

> Save this page on your phone's home screen so you can get to it in one tap.

**On your portal you'll see:**
- Your assigned jobs (date, time, property, customer, your pay per job)
- A **Clock In / Clock Out** button on each job
- Your on-site time so far
- Your paychecks (digital stubs) once your manager generates them

---

## 3. Why the Time Clock Exists

Your manager tracks location **only while you are on the clock**, and only while you're
at an assigned job. This is what protects the fairness of the system:

- **Right thing, right place:** everyone gets credit for the work they actually do.
- **No one can "clock in from home"** and claim a job — you have to be at the property.
- **Fair pay:** your paycheck is built from jobs you actually completed.

The golden rule: **Clock in when you arrive. Clock out when you're done. Be where you
say you are.**

---

## 4. How to Use the Time Clock

### Clocking In
1. Arrive at the job site.
2. Open your portal and find the job.
3. Tap **🕐 Clock In**.
4. Your phone asks for **location permission** — tap **Allow** (this is required).
5. If you're within **100 metres (1/16 mile)** of the property, you're in —
   you'll see **"ON THE CLOCK"** and a green in-zone message.

> ⚠️ If you're too far away, it won't let you clock in and will show you how far you
> are from the job. Walk to the property and try again.

### While You're On the Clock
- Your phone sends your live location to your manager so they can see you're on site.
- Keep the portal page open while you work — it keeps your location current.
- You can see your **on-site timer** and how far you are from the job.

### Clocking Out
- When the job is finished, tap **Clock Out**.
- **Location sharing stops immediately** when you clock out. That's it — no tracking
  off the clock, ever.

### After You Finish
- Tap **"Mark job done"** so the job counts toward your paycheck.

## 5. What If Something Goes Wrong

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Phone won't let me allow location** | Check phone Settings → Location → make sure it's **On** for your browser. Refresh the portal. |
| **"You're X m from the job"** | Confirm you're at the right address. Walk closer and try again. GPS can lag 10–20 m. |
| **Clock In won't finish / no signal** | Move to an area with service. If it still fails, ask your manager to clock you in and **know your exact arrival time**. |
| **Forgot my PIN** | Ask your manager for a new PIN. Don't share it with co-workers. |
| **Wrong job, wrong time** | Don't tap anything — call your manager before it changes. |
| **App/job disappeared** | Refresh the page. If still missing, contact your manager. |

**Your location / permission is never used off the clock.** If you feel that's ever not
the case, tell your manager immediately.

## 6. Pay & Paychecks

- You're paid a **flat rate per job** (your manager sets your rate).
- Each job you mark done adds to your earnings.
- Your manager **generates paychecks** on a pay period (typically two weeks).
- Your paycheck (a digital stub) shows up in your portal under **"My Paychecks."**
- Tap a paycheck to open and print it — that's your pay stub.

> Tip: keep your phone number and PIN handy. Paychecks are tied to your account, not
> to paper.

---

## 7. A Great Turnover in 60 Seconds

- **Photos:** take a photo of the property when you arrive and when you leave.
- **The checklist:** follow the assigned property's checklist (kitchen, baths, bedrooms,
  floors, linens) to 5-star standard.
- **Linens:** beds stripped, linens swapped, towels set out to spec.
- **Supplies:** restock to the property standard; tell your manager when they run low.
- **Final look:** door locked, thermostat set, lights per instructions, nothing left behind.
- **Damages/issues:** photograph and report anything broken to your manager the same day.

---

## 8. Conduct That Keeps You Hired

- Be on time. The clock proves it, and your host depends on it.
- Never share your PIN or log in for someone else.
- Never mark a job done you didn't actually do.
- Guest privacy: don't touch guest belongings beyond cleaning, and never take photos
  of personal items to share.
- Property care: treat every home like it's your own.
- Communicate: if you're running late or a job runs long, text your manager first.

---

## 9. Who to Contact

- **Time clock or account issues:** your manager immediately.
- **Job questions / schedule changes:** your manager.
- **Door access / alarms / codes:** your manager.
- **Emergency at a property** (fire, flood, lockout): call 911 first, then your manager.
